Policies

Please review our order, refund and revision policies before placing an order.

💳 Refund Policy

Custom design services require creative work to be prepared specifically for the customer. Once custom design work has started, payments are generally non-refundable.


If GRAFYX Designs is unable to fulfill an accepted order, we will discuss an appropriate refund or alternative solution with the customer.


Refund eligibility will always be considered according to the specific order and applicable consumer laws.

🔄 Revision Policy

  • Logo Design — 2 revisions
  • Social Media Post — 2 revisions
  • Poster / Advertisement — 2 revisions
  • YouTube Thumbnail — 2 revisions
  • Business Card — 2 revisions
  • Custom Design — according to project requirements

Additional revisions or major changes outside the original requirements may be charged separately.
