Policies

Please review our order, refund and revision policies.

💳 Refund Policy

Custom design services involve work created specifically for each customer. Once custom design work has started, payments are generally non-refundable.


If GRAFYX Designs cannot fulfill an accepted order, we will discuss an appropriate refund or alternative solution with the customer.


Refund decisions will also follow applicable consumer laws.

🔄 Revision Policy

  • Logo Design — 2 revisions
  • Social Media Post — 2 revisions
  • Poster / Advertisement — 2 revisions
  • YouTube Thumbnail — 2 revisions
  • Business Card — 2 revisions
  • Photo Editing — 2 revisions
  • Video / Shorts / Reels — based on project
  • Custom Design — based on project requirements

Additional revisions or major changes outside the original requirements may be charged separately.
